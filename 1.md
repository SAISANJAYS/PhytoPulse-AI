1. Components required
- Arduino Uno R3
- DHT11 temperature and humidity sensor
- Rain sensor module with VCC, GND, AO and DO pins
- Soil moisture sensor with VCC, GND and AO pins
- Green, yellow and red LEDs
- 3 × 220 Ω resistors for the LEDs
- Active buzzer
- Breadboard and jumper wires
- USB cable to connect the Arduino to your computer
2. Complete Arduino pin connections
Digital pins
Arduino Uno pin	Connect to
D2	DHT11 data (DATA/S)
D5	Green LED anode (+), through 220 Ω resistor
D6	Yellow LED anode (+), through 220 Ω resistor
D7	Red LED anode (+), through 220 Ω resistor
D8	Active buzzer positive (+)


Analog pins
Arduino Uno pin	Connect to
A0	Rain sensor DO (digital output)
A1	Soil moisture sensor AO (analog output)

This matches your existing configuration: rain detection on A0 and soil moisture readings on A1.

3. Power connections
Component pin	Arduino connection
DHT11 VCC / +	5V
DHT11 GND / −	GND
Rain sensor VCC	5V
Rain sensor GND	GND
Soil moisture sensor VCC	5V
Soil moisture sensor GND	GND
Green LED cathode (−)	GND through its own 220 Ω resistor
Yellow LED cathode (−)	GND through its own 220 Ω resistor
Red LED cathode (−)	GND through its own 220 Ω resistor
Active buzzer negative (−)	GND
